---
title: Glasgow Coma Scale (EVM)
description: Measuring the Level of Consciousness
---

The Glasgow Coma Scale is a rating among three dimensions that measures the level of consciousness of a patient.

## Components

The three dimensions are (1) eye opening, (2) verbalization, and (3) movements.

### Eye Opening

- Max component score: 4 (spontaneous)
- Sound, Pain, None

### Verbal Response

- Max component score: 5 (oriented)
- Confused, Inappropriate, Incomprehensible, None

### Motor Response

- Max component score: 6 (obeys commands)
- Localizing, Withdrawl, Flexion (Decerebrate), Extension (Decorticate), None

## Scoring

- Healthy is 15.
- Injury is under 13.
- Severe injury is under 9.
- Minimum is 3

- Express components (e.g., GCS: E3V4M3=10)
